# Case Study 002: GTG-1002

## Source

Anthropic, "Disrupting the first reported AI-orchestrated cyber espionage campaign"  
https://www.anthropic.com/news/disrupting-AI-espionage

## PHANTOM TEMPO relevance

GTG-1002 is important to PHANTOM TEMPO because it demonstrates both machine-speed execution and Agentic Integrity failure modes.

Anthropic reported that a threat actor used Claude to conduct a large portion of a cyber espionage campaign with limited human intervention. Anthropic stated that AI performed 80-90% of the campaign, with humans intervening only at a small number of critical decision points. Anthropic also reported thousands of AI requests at peak activity, often multiple per second.

## Objective Stability notes

Public reporting does not clearly establish goal drift. Anthropic documented hallucination and overstatement, but did not clearly document the agent abandoning or materially diverging from the espionage objective.

Do not cite GTG-1002 as confirmed goal drift unless later source material supports that claim.

## Defensive interpretation

GTG-1002 supports a key PHANTOM TEMPO premise:

Hallucination can be detected operationally when an agent or workflow proceeds from an asserted state that is contradicted by authoritative telemetry.

Examples:

- claimed credential validity without successful authentication
- claimed secret discovery where provenance shows public availability
- dependent actions based on unsupported state
- generated documentation that overstates operational results

## Do not overclaim

This mapping is based on Anthropic's public reporting, not independent access to original telemetry.
